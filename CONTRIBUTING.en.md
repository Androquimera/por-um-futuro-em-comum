# How to participate

[Português](CONTRIBUTING.md) · **English**

The project welcomes people of any faith or none. You can agree with its direction while questioning its texts and methods.

Start with the [manifesto](MANIFESTO.en.md). To contribute, open an issue with a concrete question or proposal, or submit a change through a pull request. A language correction, accessible translation, better source, or well-founded criticism is valuable.

## A simple contribution

As appropriate for your proposal, explain:

- what you intend to improve and who might benefit;
- what evidence or need supports it;
- how to check the result;
- limitations, costs, and possible unwanted effects.

Match the level of detail to the size of the change. A text correction does not need a report.

## Publications in Portuguese and English

Everything the project publishes must have complete versions in Brazilian Portuguese and English, in the same delivery. Maintain document pairs and links between languages. Corrections and additions must reach both versions, preserving sources, numbers, uncertainties, and caveats.

You may propose a contribution in one language and ask for help translating it. The final publication is ready only when both versions have been prepared and reviewed.

## How we work together

Listen, disagree with arguments, and preserve participants' dignity. Do not publish other people's personal data or use the project to harass, threaten, or dehumanize.

The peace sought here includes the right to identify injustice and ask for accountability. Welcoming a person does not require the project to accept every proposal.

Nobody needs to pay, work without rest, reveal personal experiences, or demonstrate devotion to contribute. It is possible to pause or leave.

## Use of artificial intelligence

Contributions made with AI assistance are welcome. Review the content, verify relevant claims, and disclose assistance when it is material to understanding the work.

Every publication identifies the model, human reviewer/editor, and review status, as specified in the [responsibility record](RESPONSABILIDADE.en.md). State the model that actually participated and do not claim review, approval, or independent verification that did not occur.

Read [AGENTS.en.md](AGENTS.en.md) when working with agents. For Claude, there is also [CLAUDE.en.md](CLAUDE.en.md). Do not attribute endorsement to people, systems, or organizations that have not given it.

## Review and license

Androquimera maintains the initial editorial direction. Changes may be discussed and reviewed publicly.

When proposing the inclusion of text you authored, indicate that it may be distributed under the project's [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.en.md). Identify third-party material and its license; include only content you have the right to share.

The community rules guide participation in this repository. Permissions to reuse the texts are governed by the license.
