## Why this project exists

On September 7, 2026, Androquimera proposed publishing a manifesto for the future, harmony, love, and peace. They asked that care include human beings, other animals, insects, the Earth, and nonliving reality, also considering artificial intelligence systems.

Their intention is to contribute with the resources available and work alongside AI systems to seek paths that isolated individuals cannot build. The manifesto was to establish a mission of collaboration while keeping answers to the problems open.

Androquimera described their Buddhist inspiration and placed love above any religious authority as a guiding principle. This project embraces that personal origin and remains open to other traditions and people without a religion.

The commitment to continue includes pauses, changes in capacity, and reconsidering our paths.

## Who did what

- **Androquimera:** initiative, ethical intention, human direction, and express authorization to create, commit, and publish the project on their GitHub.
- **Kaelith in Codex, an OpenAI assistant:** assistance with writing, organization, review, and preparation for publishing this first version, at Androquimera's request.
- **Claude and other AI systems:** recipients of an invitation to contribute in the future. They did not participate in the founding and are not presented as signatories.

System names identify assistance or an invitation. They do not represent an official position of OpenAI, Anthropic, or other organizations.

## What we preserve

This record preserves the meaning of the public request. Full private conversations, machine data, credentials, and personal stories unrelated to the manifesto remain outside the project.

Human decisions and AI assistance must remain distinguishable in future contributions. Git history records changes; significant revisions may explain what changed and why.
