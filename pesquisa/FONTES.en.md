# Sources and method of the first research stage

[Português](FONTES.md) · **English**

**Consulted: 2026-09-10 · 20 sources · Version 0.1**

Scientific reports, peer-reviewed papers, and pages from the responsible institutions were consulted. The selection covers climate attribution, biodiversity, the cryosphere, the cited episodes, systemic causes, digital infrastructure, and openness in AI. It is neither an exhaustive search nor a systematic review with a literature inclusion protocol.

The “Consultation” field describes the material actually used: citing a report does not mean every page was read or its calculations reproduced. Institutional pages summarizing studies are identified as such. Publication dates must not be confused with observed periods.

Claims are recorded in the [evidence register](EVIDENCIAS.en.md). Data from different years remain dated. Policies advocated by the project are interpretations and proposals, not a consensus attributed indiscriminately to the sources.

## Interpretation criteria

The strength of each claim depends on its type and scope. Global attribution of warming, projections of electricity demand, observations of a glacier, and a definition of open source are not interchangeable evidence.

When an episode could not be identified, it was recorded as unresolved. Consulting these sources does not amount to authenticating videos that were not provided, making geophysical predictions, or demonstrating a company's intentions.

The project's texts are its own syntheses. The project's license does not alter rights over the external documents linked here.
