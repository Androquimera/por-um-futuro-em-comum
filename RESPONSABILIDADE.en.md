## What “verified by model” means here

The phrase describes **AI-assisted checking** with a declared scope: consulting selected sources, comparing claims with their contents, reviewing units, dates, and uncertainties, and editorial checks of language pairs and links. It is not equivalent to an independent audit, reproduction of all calculations, peer review, or a guarantee of no errors.

For the data center study, the scope is recorded in [Method](pesquisa/datacenters/METODO.en.md). For earlier documents, this edition adds identification and navigation; it preserves the dates and limits of the original research without claiming a new comprehensive scientific validation. The identifier above refers to the current edition and does not retroactively attribute every past version to the same model.

## Recording human review without inventing approval

“Responsible reviewer and editor” identifies who leads the review. “Reviewed and edited by” as a completed stage should be used only when human review has actually been recorded, with a date and corresponding version or commit.

Androquimera has already directed and discussed the project. This does not prove they have read every sentence of this new edition. Until a full reading is recorded, keep that status explicit. When corrections or approval arrive, record their actual scope: review of the scope, of a section, or of the whole text.

## Rule for future publications

1. Identify each participating model and its role using available metadata; never guess the model, configuration, or Claude's participation.
2. State the date, version, checking scope, and limitations.
3. Identify the reviewer/editor by their public name and authorized profile.
4. Record the actual status of human review without turning authorship, direction, or publication authorization into certification.
5. Publish the record or a visible block linking to it in every document, in both languages.
6. Update the identification when the model or team changes. Keep the history traceable in Git.

Identifying those responsible improves transparency. The strength of a conclusion still depends on the evidence and method.
