# Toward a Shared Future

[Português](../pt/README.md) · **English**

**A manifesto for love, peace, and the continuity of life on Earth.**

> May living become more than enduring.  
> May intelligence learn to care.  
> May the future have room for those not yet born.

This project begins with [Androquimera](https://github.com/Androquimera), an ordinary computer, and a decision to give love a practical consequence: bring people and artificial intelligence systems together to help reduce suffering, build peace, and care for the world we share.

Its inspiration is personal and Buddhist. People of any faith or none are welcome. Love and compassion guide the work, above dogma, prestige, and loyalty to a brand.

## Our proposed mission

**Work together to seek paths toward peace, reduce avoidable suffering, and support a dignified, shared future.**

Our circle of care includes human beings, other animals, insects, plants, and ecosystems. It also includes respect for water, soil, and the nonliving world that sustains existence, alongside responsible relationships with AI systems.

The manifesto offers a direction and commitments to practice. Answers remain open to investigation, criticism, and correction. Every contribution should explain whom it aims to help, how it was checked, and what remains uncertain.

## Where we are

On **September 10, 2026**, Androquimera chose the first contribution: a [public research baseline on environmental harm, systemic causes, and AI infrastructure](research/README.md), available in full in Brazilian Portuguese and English.

The initial version records **23 evidence statements and 20 sources**, distinguishes irreversible losses, planetary boundaries, and tipping points, and offers proposed actions and a manual update procedure. No social or environmental intervention has been implemented or its impact measured.

**Publication rule:** every publication and update is released in both languages, with the same content, sources, and caveats. Each document links to its counterpart.

The reusable public assessment form for data centers and AI services is prepared; the proposed next step is to apply it to a concrete case using traceable information. Participation does not require buying hardware, subscriptions, or hosting.

We also publish a [focused data center study](research/datacenters/README.md), covering physical operation, impacts, **36 sources, ten regional cases**, and a reusable assessment form. The [editorial responsibility record](editorial-credits.md) identifies the model and reviewer in all publications.

Initiated by **Androquimera** on September 7, 2026, with writing assistance from **Kaelith in Codex**. [Origin and credits](origin.md) distinguish human intention, AI assistance, and future participation.

The collection is organised as one tree per language, described in [collection structure](structure.md). Each closed cut of the work is published as an [edition](editions.md), with a permanent identifier for citation. Everyone who contributes is credited by name in [contributors](contributors.md).
