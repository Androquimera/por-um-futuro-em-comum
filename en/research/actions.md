## Proposed next contribution: a public form for data centers and AI

**Update of 2026-09-10:** the [form was prepared in the data center study](datacenters/method.md). Applying it to a facility or service remains the next step; the criteria below preserve the original proposal.

A deliverable suited to our resources is a reusable document for analyzing a facility or service using public information. The first application should be chosen with Androquimera. Buying hardware or paying for hosting is not necessary.

Proposed minimum fields:

1. Facility or service, location, operator, period, and assessment boundaries.
2. Total electricity consumption and data provenance; estimates separated from measurements.
3. Operational emissions and, when available, impacts of construction and equipment manufacturing.
4. Water withdrawn and consumed, source, cooling method, seasonality, and basin conditions.
5. Effects on the electricity grid, costs passed on to others, and community participation.
6. Efficiency and absolute growth: PUE and WUE, when available, alongside totals. PUE relates facility energy to IT energy; it does not measure emissions by itself. WUE requires a declared unit and boundary.
7. Claimed benefit, demonstrated benefit, alternatives, and access conditions.
8. Source and date for each field, conflicts of interest, gaps, and opportunities to challenge the assessment.

A company's sustainability report can inform the analysis, but claims must be checked against methodology and, where possible, regulatory documents and independent data. Annual electricity certificates do not automatically describe the physical source used at each hour and location.

**Completion criterion for the next stage:** a published form with fields filled only when a source exists, explicit gaps, and conclusions limited to what the data support. This will demonstrate a contribution to transparency; reduced harm can be claimed only if subsequently measured.

## What we can do now as a community

Correcting a source, identifying a news report, translating faithfully, or clarifying a unit already improves the baseline. Through this document, we do not send messages to third parties, organize campaigns on participants' behalf, or incur expenses. Any external collaboration must begin with a concrete, authorized task.

Responsibility for changing systems should not be transferred entirely to one person's behavior. Our small-scale work can support collective decisions without requiring individual exhaustion.
