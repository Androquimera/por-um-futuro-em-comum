# Living evidence register

[Português](../../pt/pesquisa/evidencias.md) · **English**

**Version 0.1 · Editorial check: 2026-09-10**

This register separates observation or estimation, attribution, projection, definition, and institutional assessment. Published uncertainties are preserved. The absence of a range below does not imply certainty: consult the source and its method.

“Confirmed” means the limited claim is supported by the cited source; it does not mean the research was independently reproduced. Consultation dates do not automatically update the period covered by the data.

**Normative positions are not evidence statements:** love as a guiding principle, criticism of exploitation, the pursuit of peace, and a preference for open AI belong to the project's commitments. Strategies chosen from these values remain subject to evaluation of their effects and correction.
